# About

## What SZL Holdings is

**SZL Holdings** builds governed-AI infrastructure with inspectable source, bounded decision
logic, and linked evidence. Its Lean corpus has an explicitly **mixed** proof posture: locked
formulas are identified separately from open `sorry`-tagged obligations, and Lambda remains a
conjecture rather than a theorem. The architecture documents a Khipu receipt design; the exact
signature and runtime status of any artifact are stated on [Compliance](/compliance) and
[Runtime status](/status), not inferred from the design.

The thesis is narrow and falsifiable: the proposed operator [`P(x,t)`](/doctrine/puriq) describes
an action as Lambda-bounded, Yuyay-gated, HUKLLA-safe, and Khipu-receipted. Open goals remain
`sorry`-tagged. The [Evidence index](/evidence/) records pinned artifacts, while the
[Investor brief](/investors/) separates source, proof, and operational claims.
