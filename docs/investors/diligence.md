## How to read an SZL claim

Use all three dimensions before treating a claim as decision-grade:

1. **Evidence class** — `REAL`, `MEASURED`, `MODELED`, `ROADMAP`, or `UNAVAILABLE`.
2. **Operational state** — a dated observation of whether a service is available; see
   [Runtime status](/status).
3. **Proof status** — `LOCKED-PROVEN`, `MIXED`, or `CONJECTURE`; see [Proof](/proof).

No page, badge, screenshot, or source release upgrades another dimension. A build artifact does
not prove a deployed runtime; a runtime response does not prove a theorem; and a hash-linked
artifact does not prove a signature unless the exact signature is independently verifiable.
